Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ATB Autopost ("the tool") is operated by the band At The Beach, Denmark (contact: atthebeachband@gmail.com). The tool is only used to publish the band's own videos to the band's own social media accounts.

What data the tool uses

  • Video files and captions that the band itself places in its own Dropbox folder.
  • Access tokens for the band's own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok, granted by the band through the platforms' official login.
  • Basic account information returned by the platforms (account name and ID), only to confirm that the correct account is connected.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect data about other users, followers or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the band's private GitHub repository and in the band's private Dropbox folder. They are only used to publish the band's own posts.

Deletion

The band can revoke the tool's access at any time in the settings of each platform. To have any data deleted, contact atthebeachband@gmail.com.
