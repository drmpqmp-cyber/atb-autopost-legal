ATB Autopost

ATB Autopost is a small internal tool used by the Danish band At The Beach to publish the band's own videos to the band's own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok on a fixed weekly schedule.

The band places finished videos in a shared folder. The tool posts them at the scheduled time and moves them to an archive folder. It is not offered to the public and it is only used with accounts owned by the band.

Uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to upload the band's videos to the band's own YouTube channel. YouTube Terms of Service · Google Privacy Policy
